Our Story

Connecting South Sudan with Reliable Transport

Jolong Transportation was founded with a vision to provide safe, reliable, and efficient transport services across South Sudan. We understand that transportation is the backbone of economic development and community connectivity.

Our journey began with a fleet of well-maintained vehicles and a team of experienced, professional drivers. Today, we are proud to be a trusted transport partner for businesses, organizations, and individuals across the country.

We are committed to safety, punctuality, and customer satisfaction. Every trip we make is a testament to our dedication to excellence in transportation.
