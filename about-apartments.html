Our Story

Creating Homes, Building Communities

Jolong Apartments was founded with a vision to transform residential living in Juba. We believe that everyone deserves a place to call home — a space that is not just functional, but beautiful, secure, and welcoming.

Our journey began with a simple commitment: to build apartments that exceed expectations. From the quality of construction to the care we take in selecting locations, every detail is designed with our residents in mind.

Today, Jolong Apartments stands as a symbol of quality living in South Sudan. We continue to grow, guided by our core values of integrity, excellence, and community.
