Our Story

Growing South Sudan's Agricultural Future

Jolong Farming was founded with a vision to transform agriculture in South Sudan. We believe that sustainable farming practices are the key to food security, economic growth, and community prosperity.

Our journey began with a commitment to modern farming techniques, crop diversification, and livestock management. Today, we are proud to be a leading agricultural enterprise in South Sudan, supporting hundreds of local farmers and feeding thousands of families.

We work closely with communities, providing training, resources, and market access to help local farmers thrive. Together, we are building a more food-secure and prosperous South Sudan.

Our Mission

What Drives Us

Our mission is to build a sustainable agricultural future for South Sudan through modern farming, community support, and environmental stewardship.

Sustainable Agriculture

We promote environmentally friendly farming practices that protect our land and ensure long-term productivity.

Community Empowerment

We support local farmers with training, resources, and market access to build thriving rural communities.

Food Security

We work to ensure reliable food supply for South Sudanese families through diverse and resilient farming systems.
