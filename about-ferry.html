Our Story

Connecting South Sudan's Communities

Jolong Ferry Crossing was founded with a vision to provide safe, reliable, and efficient water transportation across South Sudan's rivers and waterways. We understand that ferries are lifelines for communities, connecting people to essential services and opportunities.

Our journey began with a commitment to building modern, safe ferry vessels and developing routes that serve the needs of communities, businesses, and travelers. Today, we are proud to be a trusted water transport provider in South Sudan.

We are dedicated to safety, reliability, and community service. Every crossing we make is a testament to our commitment to connecting South Sudan.
