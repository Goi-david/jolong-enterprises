Our Mission

Growing South Sudan's Future

Jolong Farming is dedicated to sustainable agricultural practices that ensure food security and economic growth for South Sudan. We believe that agriculture is the backbone of our nation's development.

Through modern farming techniques, crop diversification, and livestock management, we are building a resilient agricultural sector that benefits communities across South Sudan.

Our commitment extends beyond farming — we support local farmers with training, resources, and market access to create lasting impact.
