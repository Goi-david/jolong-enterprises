Our Story

Where Juba Dines & Unwinds

Jolong Bar & Restaurant was born from a simple vision: to create a space where the people of Juba could experience exceptional dining in an atmosphere of warmth and sophistication.

Our journey began with a passion for South Sudanese cuisine and a commitment to showcasing the rich culinary heritage of our nation. We believe that food brings people together, and every meal at Jolong is crafted to create lasting memories.

From our signature dishes inspired by traditional recipes to our handcrafted cocktails, every element of Jolong reflects our dedication to quality, authenticity, and hospitality.
