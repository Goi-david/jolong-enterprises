About Us

Building South Sudan's Future

Jolong Enterprises Limited provides reliable and efficient services designed to meet the needs of individuals, businesses, and organizations in Juba, South Sudan. We focus on safe, timely, and well-coordinated solutions across all our business divisions.

Our transportation services include the movement of goods, equipment, and supplies, ensuring secure handling and delivery. We use well-maintained vehicles suitable for different types of transport needs, including local distribution and project support services.
