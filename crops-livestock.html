Our Crops

What We Grow

We cultivate a variety of crops using sustainable and modern farming techniques to ensure food security and economic growth.

🌾 Staple Crop

Sorghum

Drought-resistant grain essential for food security and livestock feed. Rich in nutrients and versatile in use.

🌽 Staple Crop

Maize

High-yield corn variety for human consumption and animal feed. Grown using sustainable farming practices.

🌻 Cash Crop

Sunflower

Oil-producing crop for cooking oil and industrial applications. High demand in local and regional markets.

🥬 Fresh Produce

Vegetables

Fresh vegetables including tomatoes, onions, leafy greens, and peppers — supplying local markets daily.

🥜 Legume

Groundnuts

Protein-rich legume for human consumption and oil production. Improves soil fertility through nitrogen fixation.

🌿 Root Crop

Cassava

Drought-tolerant root crop providing carbohydrates for food security and income generation.

🌾 Cash Crop

Sesame

High-value oilseed crop with strong export potential. Thrives in South Sudan's climate.

🍊 Orchard

Citrus & Fruits

Oranges, mangoes, and other fruits grown in our orchards for fresh consumption and juice production.

Our Livestock

Quality Livestock

We raise healthy, high-quality livestock using modern management practices and humane animal husbandry.

🐄 Beef & Dairy

Cattle

Quality beef and dairy cattle raised on pasture-based systems. Providing meat, milk, and breeding stock to local markets.

🐐 Meat & Milk

Goats

Hardy goat breeds adapted to local conditions. Excellent source of meat, milk, and income for rural communities.

🐔 Eggs & Meat

Poultry

Modern poultry operations producing high-quality eggs and chicken meat for local consumption and markets.

🐑 Wool & Meat

Sheep

Dual-purpose sheep breeds providing quality meat and wool. Well-suited to South Sudan's pastoral regions.

🐖 Pork Production

Pigs

Modern pig farming operations producing quality pork for local and regional markets.

🐟 Aquaculture

Fish Farming

Sustainable aquaculture operations producing tilapia and catfish for local consumption and markets.
